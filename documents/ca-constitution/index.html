Constitution of the State of California

As last amended November 5, 2024 (per the source publication).

    Official text source: www.senate.ca.gov/system/files/2026-04/2025-26_california_and_us_constitution.pdf

    Summaries on this page are unofficial, written in plain language by site volunteers, and are not legal advice. When the summary and the text differ, the text controls. Check the official source before relying on any wording.
