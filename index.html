How to read the labels

Every legal claim on this site carries a label and links to its source.

  • Verified We read it in the cited source.
  • Unverified Not confirmed yet. Treat it as a lead to check.
  • Calculated We worked it out from the cited source. Check the math.

More about labels and our rules

One fact to start with

Williams is a general law city: the city says it "was made a General Law City on May 20, 1920" Verified1. That means state law sets many of the rules the city must follow.

Three layers of law explains why that matters.
