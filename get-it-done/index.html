City Hall: 810 E Street · (530) 473-2955 Verified1. City pages list different City Hall hours, so call before you go.

Business licenseApply online; pay at Finance
  • Apply: use the city's online Business License Application Verified2,3.
  • Renew or pay online: the portal marks both "coming soon" Verified3. The city's page says payment is taken at the Finance Department Verified2.
  • How long it lasts: one calendar year from the date it is issued Verified2.
  • Questions: the License Collector, (530) 235-3279, agrajeda@cityofwilliams.org Verified2.
  • More on the city's Business License page. Fees are on our Fees and rates page.
Building and encroachment permitsBoth apply online
  • Apply: the city's permit portal has a Building Permit Application and an Encroachment Permit Application Verified4.
  • Encroachment permits (work in a public street or right-of-way) are issued under Williams Municipal Code chapter 12.14. The portal asks you to notify Public Works one working day before work starts. It gives two numbers for this, (530) 473-2955 Ext. 801 and (530) 473-2519. Call 811 at least two working days before you dig Verified4.
  • Building Department: 460 8th Street, (530) 235-3276 Verified1.
  • Planning forms are in the city's forms library Verified5. See also our Planning basics.
Ask for public recordsOnline form, PDF form, or phone
  • Online: the City Clerk page has a Public Records Request form Verified6.
  • On paper: the Records Request form (PDF) Verified7.
  • Phone: City Clerk, (530) 235-3274 Verified6.
  • The city has 10 days to tell you whether it has records it can release, with up to 14 more days in unusual circumstances Verified8. See our Public Records Act guide.
Water and sewer serviceStart, stop, billing, rates
  • Start service: every new customer fills out the city's Application for Service; property owners also provide a copy of the recorded deed. Details are on the Utilities page Verified9.
  • Stop service: call Customer Service at (530) 235-3274 for a Discontinuance of Service form Verified9.
  • Billing questions: the city says email is fastest: aramirez@cityofwilliams.org, or leave a voicemail at (530) 235-3274. Allow 24 hours for an email reply and up to two days for a call back Verified9.
  • Billing office: 464 8th Street, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Verified10.
  • Rates: 2026 water and sewer rates (PDF) Verified11, explained on our Fees and rates page.
  • Shut-off policy: English (PDF) · Spanish (PDF) Verified12.
City formsOne library for every department
  • The Forms & Permits library has building, business, City Clerk, Parks and Recreation (including a Spanish registration form), special event, planning and public works forms Verified5.

Meetings: watch, read, attendCouncil, Planning and Parks commissions
  • Watch: the city's livestream and video archive Verified13.
  • Read: agendas and minutes Verified14, or our item-by-item Agenda Watch.
  • City Council: third Wednesday of each month, 6:00 p.m., Council Chambers, 810 E Street Verified15.
  • Planning Commission: fourth Monday, 6:00 p.m. Parks and Recreation Commission: first Thursday, 6:00 p.m. Verified16. Check the public notices before you go.
