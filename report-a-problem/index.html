Something is wrong on your street or next door. This page tells you which office to contact, how to reach it, and what to say. This is an unofficial guide. If we differ, the city's own pages are right.

In an emergency, call 911 Verified1.

ProblemWho to contact
Weeds, junk, abandoned cars, unsafe housingCode Enforcement, (530) 473-2533
Potholes, sidewalks, signs, street lightsPublic Works, (530) 473-2519
Water pressure or taste, plugged sewerPublic Works, (530) 473-2519
Missing garbage or recycling canNorCal Waste, (530) 473-3282
Police, not an emergencyWilliams Police, (530) 473-2661
County roads outside the cityColusa County Public Works, (530) 458-0466

Weeds, junk and other property problems (code enforcement)

Code Enforcement handles nuisances on private property. The city lists abandoned vehicles, weeds and other fire hazards, rubbish and debris, livestock, and substandard (unsafe) housing Verified2.

  • Call: the Police Services Division at (530) 473-2533, Monday through Thursday.
  • Go in person: Williams Police Department, 700 North Street, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
  • Email: info@williamspd.net.
  • App: the city's Nuisance Abatement page has a QR code for the Police Department's mobile app.

Sources: Verified2,5

Online: the city's page says you can file a complaint online. We could not find the online form on that page, so this is not confirmed Unverified2.

Streets, sidewalks, signs and street lights

Public Works takes care of city streets, street trees, traffic systems and parks Verified6. Call Public Works at (530) 473-2519 about:

  • a pothole on a city street
  • a broken sidewalk next to city property (other sidewalks may be the owner's job)
  • a missing or damaged street sign
  • a street light or traffic signal that is out. Public Works contacts PG&E, so give the exact location.
  • a flooded storm drain
  • street tree trimming or park upkeep
  • dust from a construction site

Source: Verified3

Other ways to reach Public Works: dpw@cityofwilliams.org, or the office at 735 7th Street. The Public Works contact page lists (530) 473-2955 Ext. 801 and has a web form. The city's directory lists (530) 235-3270 Ext. 801 Verified7,8.

Water and sewer problems

  • Low water pressure, bad taste, or a plugged sewer main: call Public Works at (530) 473-2519. The sewer line from the main to a home is the owner's job Verified3.
  • A question about your water bill: email aramirez@cityofwilliams.org or leave a voicemail at (530) 235-3274 Verified9.

Garbage and recycling

  • Missing garbage can or recycling bin: call NorCal Waste at (530) 473-3282.
  • Too much trash for your can: the Colusa County transfer station in Maxwell, (530) 438-2622.

Source: Verified3

Police, not an emergency

Williams Police Department, 700 North Street. Call (530) 473-2661 or email info@williamspd.net. The police Contact Us page also has a web form Verified4.

A complaint about a police officer

State law says every police department must have a way to look into complaints from the public. It must also give the public a written description of how it works (Penal Code §832.5) Verified10. When we checked on October 29, 2026, the city's police pages did not post a complaint form or that written description Verified11. Ask the department for it at (530) 473-2661 or info@williamspd.net Verified4.

County roads outside city limits

The Colusa County Road Department takes care of county roads outside city limits. Report a problem with the county's online Road Maintenance Request Form (you need to sign up first) or call County Public Works at (530) 458-0466. The county's Williams yard is at 5714 E Street, (530) 473-5301 Verified1.

A complaint about how a city or county office is run

  • The Colusa County Grand Jury page links a complaint form Verified12.
  • You can also speak to the City Council during public comment. See how to speak.

What to put in your report

The city asks for two things Verified2,5:

  1. The exact location or address.
  2. A complete description of the problem.

Our tips (not from the city): write down the date and time you reported it and who you talked to. Keep a copy of any email. Call again if nothing changes.

See also: Get it done for city phones, forms and permits, and Who decides? to find who makes the rules behind a problem.
