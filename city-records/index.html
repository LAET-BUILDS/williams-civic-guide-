City records in plain terms

What the City of Williams has decided, charges, and spends, taken from the city's own public records and written short.

Key takeaways

  • Laws: 27 ordinances and 53 resolutions, 2024 to 2026, one line each Verified1,2.
  • Fees: home water and sewer base charges are $133.60 a month now and $184.68 by 2030 Calculated3.
  • Budget: a $5.7 million General Fund in FY2026; police is about 41% of it Verified4Calculated4.

How we built these pagesPublic sources only
  • Everything comes from public city records: the agenda packets, the posted ordinances, the rates and budget PDFs, and the planning pages Verified2. Most packets are scans, and we read them with text recognition (OCR). We checked every number against the page itself.
  • Results from the Oct 21, 2026 meeting come from our notes of the official video. They are unofficial until the Council approves the minutes Verified6.
  • We put the city's words into our own words and quote only the law. We link to the city's maps and plans rather than copying them.
  • We don't name private residents. Officials are named only for their official acts.
  • This is unofficial education, not legal advice. To learn how we label claims, see Reference. To check what is on upcoming agendas, see Agenda Watch.
