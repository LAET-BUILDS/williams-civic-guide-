The Ralph M. Brown Act (open meetings law)

On this page (3 sections)
  1. Unofficial plain-language summary
  2. Using it in Williams
  3. Sources cited on this page

Official text: California Government Code, Title 5, Division 2, Part 1, Chapter 9, starting at §54950 on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov Unverified1. The official site blocked automated downloads when we built this page, so the full text is not reproduced here; open the link in a normal browser.

The California Constitution also guarantees access: "the meetings of public bodies and the writings of public officials and agencies shall be open to public scrutiny" (Art. I, §3(b)(1)) Verified2. Read Article I, Section 3.

Unofficial plain-language summary

Unofficial summary Not legal advice; the statute controls.

Topic Section What it says (summary) Label
Purpose §54950 Local agencies exist to do the people's business, and their actions and deliberations should be done openly. Verified3
Agenda posting §54954.2 Regular meeting agendas must be posted at least 72 hours before the meeting, with a brief description of each item. Generally, no action on items not on the agenda. Verified4
Public comment §54954.3 The public must have a chance to address the body on items within its jurisdiction. Reasonable time limits are allowed; criticism may not be prohibited; speakers using a translator get double time. Verified5
Special meetings §54956 Generally require 24 hours' notice. Verified6
Closed sessions §54956.7–§54957.x Allowed only for listed topics (for example, some personnel, labor negotiations, litigation, real estate). Unverified1
Reporting closed-session actions §54957.1 Certain actions taken in closed session must be reported publicly. Verified7
Enforcement §54960 Anyone can sue to stop or prevent violations. Verified8
Cure or correct §54960.1 Before suing to void an action, send a written demand: within 90 days (30 days for certain agenda violations); the body has 30 days to respond; then 15 days to sue. Verified9

"Verified" above means we read that section's text on an unofficial copy (Justia); "Unverified" means it comes from a summary or general knowledge. Check every section on leginfo before relying on it.
