California Public Records Act (CPRA)

On this page (3 sections)
  1. Unofficial plain-language summary
  2. Using it in Williams
  3. Sources cited on this page

Official text: Gov. Code §7920.000 and following Verified1. Start at §7920.000 on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov. We read the sections in the table below on that official site on October 29, 2026. The full text is not copied here; open the link to read it.

The California Constitution says the people have "the right of access to information concerning the conduct of the people's business" (Art. I, §3(b)(1)) Verified2. Read Article I, Section 3.

Unofficial plain-language summary

Unofficial summary Not legal advice; the statute controls.

Topic Section What it says (summary) Label
Response time §7922.535 The agency must decide within 10 days whether it has disclosable records and tell you; it may extend by up to 14 days in unusual circumstances. Verified3,4
Partial release §7922.525 You may inspect records during office hours. Any part that can reasonably be separated must be released after the exempt parts are removed. Verified5,6
Help with your request §7922.600 The agency must help you identify the records that answer your request, describe the technology and physical location of the records, and suggest ways around practical problems that would block access. Verified7
Court enforcement §7923.000, §7923.100 You can sue to enforce the Act; a successful requester can get court costs and attorney's fees. Verified8,9,10

"Verified" means we read the section ourselves; the rows marked with a leginfo source were read on the official site. The statute controls, so quote the section itself in a request.

Using it in Williams

  • Send requests to the City Clerk, 810 E Street. The City Clerk page has an online Public Records Request form Verified11, and there is a paper request form (PDF) Verified12. Phone: (530) 235-3274 Verified13,11.
  • Step 6 of The Method suggests records that show how a rule is enforced.
