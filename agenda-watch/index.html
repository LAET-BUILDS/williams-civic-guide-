Agenda Watch

Every City Council meeting from October 21, 2026 on, item by item, in plain language. The goal is not to be neutral. It's to show you what to look at before the council votes, and what it did afterward.

  • Flags mark items that spend money, award or change contracts, change staff pay or benefits, or change fees and rates you pay. Consent-calendar items that deserve a look are flagged too, because the consent calendar passes in one vote with no discussion.
  • Every fact links to its source, usually the official agenda packet or the meeting video. If we can't source it, it isn't here.
  • Votes are unofficial until the council approves the minutes. We copy them from the video with the timestamp so you can check.
  • Private residents who speak at meetings are not named. City officials and staff are.

Do your own watch

  1. Agendas are posted on the official agendas and minutes page Verified2. For a regular meeting, state law requires posting at least 72 hours ahead Verified4.
  2. Open the packet PDF and read the staff report behind each item, not just the agenda title. On October 21, 2026, the money in items 15 and 18 appeared only in the staff reports Verified3.
  3. Speak during public comment, or ask for records under the Public Records Act.
  4. Watch or re-watch meetings on the video portal Verified5.
