This page lists how the Williams City Council voted on rates and fees, contracts, pay, the budget, ordinances, appointments and land use. Each row comes from the city's own minutes, and each links to the page of the PDF it comes from. Members are named the way the minutes name them. To find who decides a particular issue, see Who decides?.

Unofficial. The minutes are the official record, and we copied from scanned pages; if a row here and the minutes disagree, the minutes win.

What this covers. Minutes of 45 meetings, 31 regular and 14 special, from January 17, 2024 through September 16, 2026. We list 134 decisions from 34 of those meetings; 131 have a roll call. Missing: minutes for March 20, 2024, June 19, 2024 and the October 8, 2024 special meeting (see Gaps). The October 21, 2026 minutes are not posted yet.

How we built this

  • Source. The City of Williams posts council minutes inside the agenda packet for the next meeting, as a draft to approve. We used those drafts, plus the signed minutes the city posts for January and February 2024 Verified1. The council sometimes corrects minutes when it approves them. Where a correction changes a row here, the row says so.
  • What we included. Roll-call votes on rates, fees and taxes; contracts and agreements; pay and personnel; the budget and spending; ordinances; appointments; and land use. Consent-calendar votes are listed only when the consent items included a contract, a rate or pay.
  • What we left out. Approving minutes, proclamations, accepting reports, scheduling and continuing items, special-event permits, direction to staff, election-administration resolutions, censure resolutions, and items about a single private property. We also leave out the names of residents: where the minutes name a resident appointed to a commission, we write "[one resident]".
  • How votes are recorded. The minutes list members under Ayes, Noes, Abstain and Absent. We copied those lists. Where the minutes' own tally (for example "5-0") does not match the names listed, we follow the names and add a note.
  • Errors. We read the scans with text recognition and checked every row by eye. If you find a mistake, the linked page of the minutes is the place to check.
