Oct 21, 2026: City Council results

From our notes of the official video. Unofficial until the Council approves the minutes.

  • City Manager contract amendment (item 18): passed 4-1, Troughton no Verified3.

Severance, if she is let go without cause, rises from three months' pay to up to six by Feb 2028 Verified4. Vote at 01:40:32 in the video. Council Member Parsons' answer was inaudible; the 4-1 count implies an aye Unverified3.

  • Item 17. A Ford F-450 for $94,866.92 (water fund) and an F-350 for $62,710.59 (sewer fund) Verified4, $157,577.51 together Calculated4. Vote at 01:14:37.
