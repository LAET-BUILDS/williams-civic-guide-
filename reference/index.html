Disclaimer

The full statement of what this site is, and is not.

Unofficial education, not legal advice. This site is written by residents. It is not the City of Williams, and no lawyer has reviewed it. Check every claim against its source. For official information, go to cityofwilliams.org.

It teaches you which questions to ask; it does not tell you whether any rule is valid. Every legal claim is labeled Verified or Unverified and links to its source. For your own situation, talk to a licensed California attorney.

Not affiliated with the City of Williams, Colusa County, or any government agency.

How we label claims

How to read the labels
Verified
We read this in the cited source on the date shown on the Reference page. For statutes, some “Verified” items were read on a reputable unofficial copy (Justia or FindLaw); the Reference page says which.
Unverified
Not confirmed: from a summary, a search result, general knowledge, or a source we could not open directly. Treat it as a lead to check, not a fact.
Calculated
We calculated this from the cited source (for example, a meeting date from the regular schedule). Check the math and the source.

Our rules: every legal claim gets a label and a source; we do not invent legal facts; when a source is an unofficial copy (a code mirror, Justia or FindLaw) we say so; we never bypass a website's bot protection. Where an official site blocked automated access, we link to it and label what we could not read as Unverified. No lawyer has reviewed this site.
