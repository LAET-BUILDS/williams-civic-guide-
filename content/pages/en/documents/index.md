# Founding documents

The two constitutions are the top two [layers of law](/layers/) that every Williams rule must fit under. Full texts are copied from official government sources; summaries are **unofficial** and in plain language.

## Good places to start

- U.S. Constitution, [Article VI](/documents/us-constitution/#article-vi) (federal law is supreme) and the [Fourteenth Amendment](/documents/us-constitution/#amendment-xiv) (due process, equal protection).
- California Constitution, [Article I](/documents/ca-constitution/article-i/) (rights) and [Article XI, §7](/documents/ca-constitution/article-xi/#article-xi-sec-7) (city ordinances may not conflict with general laws).
