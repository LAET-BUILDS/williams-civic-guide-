# The Ralph M. Brown Act (open meetings law)

**Official text:** California Government Code, Title 5, Division 2, Part 1, Chapter 9, starting at [§54950 on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=54950) [[U:leginfo-brown]]. The official site blocked automated downloads when we built this page, so the full text is **not** reproduced here; open the link in a normal browser.

The California Constitution also guarantees access: "the meetings of public bodies and the writings of public officials and agencies shall be open to public scrutiny" (Art. I, §3(b)(1)) [[V:senate-constitutions]]. [Read Article I, Section 3](/documents/ca-constitution/article-i/#article-i-sec-3).

## Unofficial plain-language summary

<p><span class="tag tag-summary">Unofficial summary</span> Not legal advice; the statute controls.</p>

| Topic | Section | What it says (summary) | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | §54950 | Local agencies exist to do the people's business, and their actions and deliberations should be done openly. | [[U:leginfo-brown]] |
| Agenda posting | §54954.2 | Regular meeting agendas must be posted at least 72 hours before the meeting, with a brief description of each item. Generally, no action on items not on the agenda. | [[U:leginfo-brown]] |
| Public comment | §54954.3 | The public must have a chance to address the body on items within its jurisdiction. Reasonable time limits are allowed; criticism may not be prohibited; speakers using a translator get double time. | [[V:brown-54954-3]] |
| Special meetings | §54956 | Generally require 24 hours' notice. | [[U:leginfo-brown]] |
| Closed sessions | §54956.7-§54957.x | Allowed only for listed topics (for example, some personnel, labor negotiations, litigation, real estate). | [[U:leginfo-brown]] |
| Reporting closed-session actions | §54957.1 | Certain actions taken in closed session must be reported publicly. | [[U:leginfo-brown]] |
| Enforcement | §54960 | Anyone can sue to stop or prevent violations. | [[U:leginfo-brown]] |
| Cure or correct | §54960.1 | Before suing to void an action, send a written demand: within 90 days (30 days for certain agenda violations); the body has 30 days to respond; then 15 days to sue. | [[V:brown-54960-1]] |

"Verified" above means we read that section's text on an unofficial copy (Justia); "Unverified" means it comes from a summary or general knowledge. Check every section on leginfo before relying on it.
