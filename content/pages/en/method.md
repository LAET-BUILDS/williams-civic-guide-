# The Method: seven steps to check a city rule

Use these steps for any section of the Williams Municipal Code. They help you **ask good questions and find official sources**. They do not give you a verdict on whether a rule is valid. Only a court can decide that. *Unofficial education, not legal advice.*

**The seven steps:** 1. Find it · 2. Read it · 3. Check state law · 4. Check rights · 5. Check court cases · 6. Check enforcement · 7. Ask.

## How to use the checklist

- Tick each item as you finish it. Write the section you are checking at the top, and anything you could not answer in the **Still unknown** box at the end.
- Your ticks and notes are saved **only in this browser** on this device, using local storage. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. No account, no cookies, no tracking.
- To start over, use **Clear my checklist** at the bottom.
- Every step has a source line. Labels show whether we have <span class="tag tag-v">Verified</span> the source or not (<span class="tag tag-u">Unverified</span>).

## Tips

- Write down **where** you read each thing and **when**. Codes and statutes change.
- Treat <span class="tag tag-u">Unverified</span> items on this site as leads, not facts. Confirm them on the official source.
- If a deadline might apply to you (for example, to challenge a decision), talk to a licensed attorney early. Deadlines can be short.
