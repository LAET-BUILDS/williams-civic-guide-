<h2 id="labels">How we label claims</h2>

{{legend}}

**Our rules:** every legal claim gets a label and a source; we do not invent legal facts; when a source is an unofficial copy (a code mirror, Justia or FindLaw) we say so; we never bypass a website's bot protection. Where an official site blocked automated access, we link to it and label what we could not read as Unverified. No lawyer has reviewed this site.
