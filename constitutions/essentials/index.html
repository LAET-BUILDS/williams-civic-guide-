The essentials: 5 lessons to start with

Short on time? Start with these five lessons. They cover the rights you are most likely to use with the City of Williams. Each one has a short quiz at the end. All 68 lessons stay in the full course.

  2. Step 2 California Article I (part 2): Speech, petition, open government and religionWhy open meetings and public records are a right in California.Lesson 2 · Speech, religion and public comment
