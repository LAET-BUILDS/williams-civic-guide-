California Article XIII: Taxation, the base rules

Lesson 24 of 68 · Taxes, budgets, fees and utilities · 26 min read

Article XIII is the original taxation article. Later initiatives (XIII A, XIII B, XIII C, XIII D) sit on top of it.

Key takeaways

  • "All property is taxable and shall be assessed at the same percentage of fair market value" unless the constitution or federal law says otherwise (§1(a)).
  • §3 lists exemptions, including government property, public schools and "Buildings, land on which they are situated, and equipment used exclusively for religious worship."
  • The Legislature "may not impose taxes for local purposes but may authorize local governments to impose them" (§24(a)), and may not take a city's own tax proceeds (§24(b)).
  • To challenge a state tax, pay first, then sue (§32).

Section 1: Uniformity

Section 3: Exemptions

Section 16: Assessment appeals

Our reading: if you think your Colusa County assessment is wrong, §16 is the basis for the county assessment appeals board. Deadlines are set by statute.

Section 24: Local taxes belong to local governments

"The Legislature may not reallocate, transfer, borrow, appropriate, restrict the use of, or otherwise use the proceeds of any tax imposed or levied by a local government solely for the local government's purposes" (§24(b)) Verified1.

Section 25.5: Property tax shares protected

"On or after November 3, 2004, the Legislature shall not enact a statute" that reduces the local share of property tax revenue in a county below the 2004 statutory share, with exceptions (§25.5(a)) Verified1.

Sections 29–32: Sharing, no surrender, pay first

Section 35: Public safety sales tax

"The protection of the public safety is the first responsibility of local government" (§35(a)(2)) Verified1.

In Williams

  • Williams' share of sales tax and property tax is set by statute within §§24, 25.5 and 29 Verified1.
  • Churches and schools in Williams don't pay property tax on qualifying property (§3) Verified1.

Check yourself

Pick an answer for each question. You will see right away if it is right, and why.

Question 1. Can the Legislature impose a tax for a city's local purposes?

Show the answer and why

No, but it may authorize the city to impose one (§24(a)) Verified1.

Question 2. What does §31 say about the power to tax?

Show the answer and why

It "may not be surrendered or suspended by grant or contract" Verified1.

Question 3. How do you challenge a state tax you think is illegal?

Show the answer and why

Pay it, then sue to recover it (§32) Verified1.

Question 4. Scenario: Suppose the Legislature wants to take tax money Williams levies for its own purposes and use it for a state program. Can it?

Show the answer and why

Answer: No; §24(b) bars the Legislature from taking a city's own tax proceeds.
