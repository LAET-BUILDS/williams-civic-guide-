California Article XX: Miscellaneous, including the oath every city employee takes

Lesson 39 of 68 · Alcohol, schools, water and other state rules · 8 min read

On this page (6 sections)
  1. Section 3: The oath
  2. Other sections
  3. Every Article XX section

  5. Check yourself

Article XX is a collection of miscellaneous rules. The one every Williams resident should know is the oath: every council member and city employee swears to support both constitutions.

Key takeaways

  • Every Williams council member and city employee swears to "support and defend the Constitution of the United States and the Constitution of the State of California" (§3).
  • The oath's second paragraph (a loyalty disclaimer) was held invalid in Vogel (1967).
  • The State has "the exclusive right and power to license and regulate" alcohol (§22).

Section 3: The oath

Who takes it. "Members of the Legislature, and all public officers and employees, executive, legislative, and judicial, except such inferior officers and employees as may be by law exempted" Verified1. "Public officer and employee" "includes every officer and employee of the State, ... every county, city, city and county, district, and authority" Verified1.

The struck paragraph. "The trial court held that the second paragraph of the oath is invalid, and granted the injunction. We agree with this determination" (Vogel v. County of Los Angeles (1967) 68 Cal.2d 18) Verified2. The text still appears in the published constitution Verified1.

Our reading: the oath doesn't authorize an official to ignore a statute they think unconstitutional; Lockyer says local officials must enforce state statutes until a court rules (Lockyer v. City and County of San Francisco (2004) 33 Cal.4th 1055) Verified3. It's a promise to work within both constitutions.

Other sections

Section 22: Alcohol

The State "shall have the exclusive right and power to license and regulate the manufacture, sale, purchase, possession and transportation of alcoholic beverages within the State" (§22) Verified1. Compare the Twenty-First Amendment. Our reading: Williams can use zoning for where bars and stores go, but licensing is the State's.

In Williams

  • Oath on file. Ask the city clerk for the signed oaths of council members Verified4.
  • Alcohol licenses come from the State, not the city (§22) Verified1.

Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then open each question.

Do city employees take the Article XX oath?

Yes; it covers every officer and employee of every city, unless exempted by law Verified1.

What did Vogel v. County of Los Angeles hold?

That the oath's second paragraph is invalid Verified2.

Who licenses alcohol sales in California?

The State (§22) Verified1.
