California's other articles: labor, interest, transportation money, redistricting, housing, stem cells, and repealed articles

Lesson 41 of 68 · Alcohol, schools, water and other state rules · 24 min read

These articles matter less often to a Williams resident, but each is law. Every section is listed with its heading and a link to the full text.

Key takeaways

  • XIV lets the Legislature set minimum wages and gives it "plenary power" over workers' compensation.
  • XIX and XIX A–D lock gas tax and other vehicle revenue to transportation uses, which funds local streets.
  • XXI hands state legislative and congressional districts to a Citizens Redistricting Commission.
  • XXXIV requires local voter approval before a public body develops a "low rent housing project."
  • Articles VIII, XVII, XXIII through XXVIII were repealed; XXIX to XXXIII were never adopted.

Section headings and status come from the Senate's published text Verified1.

Vandermost v. Bowen (2012) held "the Commission's certified map is clearly the most appropriate map to be used in the 2012 state Senate elections even if the proposed referendum qualifies for the ballot" (Vandermost v. Bowen (2012) 53 Cal.4th 421) Verified2. Article XXI covers state and congressional districts, not Williams council elections Verified1.

Article XXII: Architectural and engineering services

Cities, including Williams, "shall be allowed to contract with qualified private entities for architectural and engineering services for all public works of improvement" Verified1. See Article VII.

Check yourself

Which article requires voter approval for low rent public housing projects?

Who draws state legislative districts?
